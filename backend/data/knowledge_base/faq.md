# Medics Online — Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How does Medics Online match me to a job?**
A: You upload your CV. Dr. Eezy extracts your profession (Doctor or Nurse), specialty,
years of experience, and skills, then compares this against open job postings using
semantic similarity, ranking the best-fitting roles first.

**Q: I'm a nurse — will I see doctor vacancies in my matches?**
A: No. Matching is always filtered by profession first (Doctor vs Nurse), then ranked by
specialty and skill overlap within that profession.

**Q: Can Dr. Eezy help with scheduling once I'm hired?**
A: Yes. Once you're placed, hospital/clinic administrators can use Dr. Eezy to generate a
balanced work roster for their team and print it to PDF.

**Q: What documents should I upload?**
A: A CV in PDF format listing your qualifications, registration number (HPCSA for doctors,
SANC for nurses), specialty, years of experience, and key skills.

**Q: Is my data used to train external AI models?**
A: No. Matching runs entirely on locally-hosted, open-source models — your CV data does
not leave the Medics Online infrastructure.
